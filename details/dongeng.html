Social Media Content – Dongeng Kopi Yogyakarta

Dongeng Kopi is a local coffee shop in Yogyakarta that emphasizes a warm atmosphere, storytelling, and meaningful customer experience. In this project, I worked as a focusing on creating short-form video content for Instagram Reels to strengthen brand identity and increase audience engagement.

1. Content Objective

  • Increase brand awareness through visual storytelling
  • Highlight the ambience and uniqueness of Dongeng Kopi
  • Promote weekday visits with subtle, non-hard-selling content
  • Build emotional connection with the audience

2. Content Strategy

The content was designed using a soft-selling approach, focusing on atmosphere, mood, and storytelling rather than direct promotion. Visual elements such as wooden textures, stairs, and warm lighting were intentionally highlighted to represent the brand’s identity.

  • Short-form vertical video optimized for Instagram Reels
  • Minimal text overlay to maintain visual focus
  • Story-driven caption aligned with the brand message
  • Weekday-focused content to attract non-peak visitors

3. Content Creation

The video content showcases the interior and atmosphere of Dongeng Kopi using smooth camera movement and natural lighting. Text overlays such as “Weekdays” and “Bebiji Baik Tumbuh Baik” were added to reinforce brand values and messaging.

  • Instagram Reels (vertical format)
  • Ambience-focused visual composition
  • Text overlay for brand identity and storytelling

4. Platform & Tools

  • Platform: Instagram
  • Content Format: Reels
  • Editing Tools: Capcut

5. Branding & Visual Consistency

Visual consistency was maintained by using warm tones, minimalist typography, and calm pacing. This approach supports Dongeng Kopi’s brand image as a comfortable and reflective space, aligning with the tagline “Bebiji Baik Tumbuh Baik.”

6. Impact & Value

  • Strengthened brand storytelling through visual content
  • Improved audience engagement with relatable and calm visuals
  • Enhanced brand positioning as a cozy and meaningful coffee space
  • Content suitable for long-term brand building, not only short-term promotion
