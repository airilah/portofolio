Visual Branding & Sticker Design – FAST 2024

FAST 2024 (Festival Ajang Sinergitas Teknologi 2024) is a technology-based event that targets young audiences and student communities. The sticker designs shown on this website were created as part of a visual branding strategy to support digital campaigns, social media promotion, and event identity.

1. Branding Objective

  • Strengthen FAST 2024 visual identity across digital platforms
  • Create engaging and recognizable visual assets
  • Attract young and tech-oriented audiences
  • Support event promotion through shareable content

2. Visual Concept

The sticker design combines futuristic elements, gaming-style visuals, and a friendly mascot character to represent technology, innovation, and collaboration. The use of dark backgrounds, star elements, and vibrant colors reinforces the modern and digital atmosphere of FAST 2024.

  • Futuristic and technology-themed visuals
  • Consistent use of FAST 2024 logo and mascot
  • Bold typography for clear readability
  • Dynamic composition suitable for digital use

3. Content Strategy Application

From a content strategy perspective, these stickers were designed to be flexible and adaptable for multiple digital formats. They can be used as Instagram Stories, Reels overlays, feed posts, event announcements, and digital merchandise.

  • Instagram Stories & Highlights
  • Feed posts and promotional banners
  • Event countdown and announcement visuals
  • Digital stickers for audience engagement

4. Reference & Tools

  • Reference: Pinterest
  • Editing Tools: Canva

5. Branding Value

The stickers function as more than decorative elements. They act as strategic branding tools that help maintain visual consistency, increase brand recall, and enhance audience engagement throughout the FAST 2024 campaign.

6. Impact & Results

  • Improved visual consistency across FAST 2024 digital content
  • Higher audience engagement through attractive and shareable visuals
  • Stronger brand recognition among event participants
  • Effective support for social media and digital campaign activities
