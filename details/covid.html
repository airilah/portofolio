Short Video Storytelling – COVID-19

This short-form video content presents a reflective narrative about the COVID-19 pandemic, which impacted daily life for more than two years. The video opens with a simple yet powerful visual — a dark background with the text “COVID-19” — setting an emotional tone and immediately capturing audience attention.

1. Content Objective

  • Raise awareness about the emotional and social impact of the pandemic
  • Create relatable content based on shared public experience
  • Encourage audience reflection and emotional engagement
  • Strengthen storytelling-based content strategy

2. Content Strategy

The content applies an emotional storytelling approach, focusing on feelings of frustration, fatigue, and reflection experienced during the pandemic period. Instead of using complex visuals, the strategy relies on minimalism to emphasize the message.

  • Minimalist visual design to support emotional delivery
  • Strong opening hook using bold typography
  • Relatable narrative aligned with audience experience
  • Short-form format optimized for Instagram Reels

3. Content Creation

The video uses a short duration and simple composition to maintain viewer focus on the message. The dark background symbolizes uncertainty and isolation, while the text overlay functions as a narrative anchor for the storyline.

  • Vertical video format (9:16)
  • Text-based storytelling
  • Emotion-driven pacing

4. Platform & Tools

  • Platform: Instagram Reels
  • Content Type: Awareness & Reflective Content
  • Editing Tools: Capcut & VN

5. Audience Impact

This type of content encourages audience interaction through emotional resonance rather than direct calls to action. Viewers are more likely to engage through comments, shares, and saves because the content reflects a shared global experience.

6. Strategic Value

  • Builds strong emotional connection with the audience
  • Supports long-term personal or brand storytelling
  • Enhances engagement through relatability
  • Suitable for awareness campaigns and reflective social media content
