Visual Identity & ID Card Design – FAST 2024

The FAST 2024 ID Card was designed as part of the visual branding system for Festival Ajang Sinergitas Teknologi 2024. Beyond its functional role as an identification tool for committee members, this ID card serves as a strategic visual asset that strengthens the overall brand identity of the event.

1. Design Objective

  • Create a strong and professional visual identity for event committees
  • Ensure consistent branding across offline and digital platforms
  • Represent the technological and youthful spirit of FAST 2024
  • Support documentation and promotional content creation

2. Visual Concept

The design combines bold typography, vibrant color contrast, and game-inspired graphic elements to reflect innovation and creativity. The layout clearly presents essential information such as name, role, and affiliation while maintaining a visually engaging composition.

  • High-contrast color palette for clear readability
  • Dynamic composition inspired by digital and gaming visuals
  • Consistent use of FAST 2024 logo and mascot
  • Clean information hierarchy for functional clarity

3. Branding & Content Strategy

From a content strategy perspective, the ID card design is optimized to be visually appealing when captured in photos and videos. This allows it to function effectively as part of behind-the-scenes content, event documentation, and social media storytelling.

  • Suitable for event documentation posts
  • Supports Instagram Stories and Reels content
  • Enhances brand consistency in digital campaigns

4. Reference & Tools

  • Reference: Pinterest
  • Editing Tools: Canva

5. Strategic Value

This ID card design reinforces professionalism while maintaining a creative and approachable visual tone. It helps strengthen audience perception of FAST 2024 as a well-organized, modern, and technology-driven event.

6. Impact & Application

  • Improved visual consistency across FAST 2024 materials
  • Stronger brand recognition during the event
  • Effective integration with social media and documentation content
  • Enhanced professional image of the organizing committee
