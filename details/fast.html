Promotional Video & Digital Campaign FAST 2024

FAST 2024 (Festival Ajang Sinergitas Teknologi) is a campus-based technology event consisting of seminars, competitions, and a grand final. In this project, I was responsible for planning, creating, and managing digital content as a focusing on increasing brand awareness, audience engagement, and event participation through social media platforms.

1. Content Strategy & Planning

The content strategy was designed to ensure consistent communication and strong audience engagement throughout the event timeline. Content was structured into three main phases:

  • Pre-Event: teaser videos, registration countdown, and informational content.
  • Event Day: real-time documentation, highlights, and behind-the-scenes content.
  • Post-Event: recap videos, appreciation posts, and event documentation.

2. Content Creation

The main focus of content production was short-form video optimized for Instagram Reels. Visual storytelling techniques were used to highlight event atmosphere, participant enthusiasm, and key moments from seminars and competitions.

  • Promotional video for event registration
  • Countdown and “H-1 Close Registration” content
  • Event highlight videos (Seminar & Grand Final)
  • Photo and video documentation for branding purposes

3. Platform & Tools

  • Platform: Instagram
  • Content Format: Reels, Feed Posts and Stories
  • Design & Editing: Canva and Capcut

4. Performance Analysis

Content performance was evaluated based on reach, engagement, and audience response. Insights from each post were used to optimize posting schedules, content format, and messaging to achieve better engagement throughout the campaign.

5. Visual Branding & Consistency

All content was designed to maintain visual consistency with FAST 2024 branding, including color schemes, typography, and tone of communication. This helped strengthen brand identity and ensure a cohesive digital presence across all posts.

6. Impact & Results

  • Improved event visibility through consistent social media promotion.
  • Increased audience engagement before and during the event.
  • Clear and structured digital documentation of FAST 2024 activities.
  • Strengthening participant presence and event branding.
